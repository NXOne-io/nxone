HOW IT WORKS
  Set one up from an invoice you have already sent and it copies the customer,
  the lines and the terms. Choose weekly, fortnightly, monthly, quarterly or
  yearly, and pause or stop it whenever you like.

  When one comes round it appears under Ready to make, and on Today. Pressing
  the button creates a DRAFT with the next number and the scheduled dates.
  Nothing is ever sent on its own, because an invoice going out unread is how a
  wrong amount reaches a customer.

  Two awkward cases are handled. A retainer billed on the 31st goes out on the
  28th in February and returns to the 31st in March, rather than drifting
  earlier every month. And if the app has not been opened for three months, you
  get the one invoice that is due and a note saying how many runs were skipped,
  instead of a pile of backdated bills.
