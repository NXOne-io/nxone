PLANS REWORKED, COPY CORRECTED, AND INSIGHTS
============================================

THE CHANGE YOU ASKED FOR
  Free now includes an account, a held copy of your work, and syncing across
  your devices, up to 2,000 records. Losing somebody's books because they
  cleared their browser would lose the person permanently, and holding a few
  thousand small records costs us almost nothing.

  Pro is now what actually costs money to run: email reminders that arrive
  whether or not you open the app, and reading a photographed bill. Plus no
  limit on what is held.

  Onboarding has a fourth step offering an account, with "use this device only"
  as a visible alternative rather than a hidden one.

THE COPY HAD TO CHANGE WITH IT
  "Nothing ever leaves your device" stopped being true the moment most people
  have an account. Every such claim has been corrected, in the home page, the
  layout description, the footer, the sidebar, two tool pages and the privacy
  policy, which now says plainly what is held, why, and what is never done with
  it. This matters more than it looks: a privacy claim that quietly stops being
  true is the kind of thing that ends a company's reputation in one article.

INSIGHTS
  Income and margin movements, costs that jumped, customers who pay late, one
  customer holding too much of what you are owed, cash running out, overdue
  money that dwarfs your bank balance, and invoices left as drafts.

  Each one carries the figures it came from, because "margin is down" is
  useless without the two numbers behind it. Nothing appears unless it passes a
  threshold: a 2% wobble is not an insight.
