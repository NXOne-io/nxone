WHY THERE IS NO AI IN IT
  An AI CFO normally means sending someone's accounts to a model provider.
  That costs money per question, gives a different answer each time, and
  contradicts the promise on your own home page.

  People ask about their money in a small number of ways, and those are precise
  queries rather than language problems. So the questions are matched on the
  device and answered by the same engine that produces your reports. The result
  is free, instant, identical every time, and nothing leaves the browser.

  A model layer can be added later for phrasing and odd questions, with a clear
  warning that the question is being sent away. That stays your decision.

WHAT IT ANSWERS
  Profit, revenue, expenses and biggest costs, for any period
  Cash, runway, and whether you can afford a given monthly cost
  Who owes you, how much is overdue, who is consistently late
  What you owe suppliers, and whether your cash covers it
  Tax collected, and when your next filing is due
  Your biggest customers, and this month against last

  Every answer shows what the question was understood to mean, so a misread is
  obvious, and links to the screen where the figure can be checked. When a
  question is not understood it says so and offers what it can answer, rather
  than producing a confident wrong number.
