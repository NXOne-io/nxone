WHAT THESE DO
  Onboarding: three short questions, then a screen listing what was configured.
  New visitors are pointed at it instead of landing on an empty dashboard.

  Sample data: a made-up business with three customers, six invoices at
  different ages including two overdue, three months of expenses and a repeating
  invoice that is due. Clearly marked, and clearing it leaves your own work
  untouched.

  Email reminders: off unless asked for, sent Monday mornings, and never sent
  when there is nothing to say. There is a preview button so nobody has to wait
  a week to see what it looks like.

TWO THINGS FIXED ALONG THE WAY
  The sidebar read local storage while rendering on the server, which caused a
  hydration error on every page. It now waits for the first paint.

  When syncing is paused because a plan does not include it, the panel used to
  say "everything here is on your account", which was untrue at exactly the
  moment it mattered. It now says syncing is paused and the device is the only
  copy.
