FIX: claims about privacy that are no longer true
=================================================

WHAT I MISSED
  When plans changed so that a free account holds a copy of your work, I
  corrected the obvious places and searched for the exact phrases I remembered
  writing. Several more said the same thing in different words, and they are
  live now:

    Home page   "Nothing is uploaded. Your customer list and your prices never
                 reach us. There is nowhere for them to go."
    Home page   "No account to make."
    Social image "Free. No sign-up. Nothing uploaded."
    About page  "There is no upload, no account, and nowhere for your data to go."
    Four tool page descriptions, which also feed Google.

  All of these were true a week ago and are not true now. The social image is
  the one that would have travelled furthest, since it appears every time
  somebody shares a link.

WHAT THEY SAY NOW
  "Free, and your work is kept safe" rather than "Free, no sign-up".
  "Your work is kept safe: a free account holds a copy, so a cleared browser or
  a lost laptop is not a lost year of invoices."
  "Start without an account: open it, type, download. Sign up when you want the
  same work on your phone as well."

  Both halves are true, and the second is still a real difference from every
  competitor, which all demand an email before showing you anything.

HOW I CHECKED THIS TIME
  I wrote a sweep that looks for the shape of the claim rather than the words:
  never leaves, nothing uploaded, no account, stays on your device, only copy.
  It now reports 17 mentions, all of them accurate. Worth re-running whenever
  this area changes again.
