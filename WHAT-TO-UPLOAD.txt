BANK STATEMENTS
  Upload the CSV your bank gives you. We work out which column is which, and
  which way round the dates are, by looking at the whole file rather than one
  row. Money in is matched to invoices by amount, name and invoice number;
  money out gets a suggested category from the description. Everything is shown
  with a reason and you confirm before anything is recorded. Lines we cannot
  read are listed with why, never dropped quietly.

RECEIPTS
  Attach a photo of a bill to an expense. It is shrunk in the browser and kept
  with the expense. Reading it automatically needs a service, which is not
  connected: the app says so plainly rather than leaving the fields empty for no
  apparent reason. To connect it later:
      npx wrangler secret put GOOGLE_VISION_KEY
      set RECEIPT_PROVIDER=google-vision

WHAT YOU OWE
  Unpaid bills in the order worth settling, with the line drawn where your cash
  runs out, so you can see the gap rather than discover it at the bank.

OPENING BALANCES, WHICH TURNED OUT TO MATTER
  Testing payables showed cash as a large negative number. The cause was real
  rather than cosmetic: NXOne only knows about money that has passed through it,
  so a business that existed before looks permanently overdrawn. You can now
  enter what was in the bank when you started, in your business details, and it
  posts properly to the ledger as capital.
