TODAY
  Cash, owed to you, what you owe and the next filing, then a list of things
  that need doing, ordered by urgency. Nothing appears unless there is
  something to act on: a quiet day shows an empty screen on purpose.
  It covers overdue customers, unpaid bills, filings due or missed, cash
  running out, and quieter things such as documents left as drafts or invoices
  with no due date.

FILING CALENDAR
  Tick what you are registered for, including the QRMP scheme for Indian GST,
  and the dates are worked out for you: GSTR-1 for May is the 11th of June,
  GSTR-3B the 20th, and so on. UK, UAE, Singapore and Australia are covered too.
  Nothing appears for a registration you have not ticked, because a calendar
  full of irrelevant dates teaches people to ignore all of them.

  Every deadline is shown with the authority it comes from, and the page says
  plainly that dates get extended and positions differ, so check what matters.
